Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What data the app uses

Kinoviq helps you discover movies and plan viewing schedules. It uses a movie catalog included in the installed app. No registration, account sign-in, or transfer of personal information is required to use these features.

What is stored on your device

The app may save the movie nights you create, selected movies, dates, start times, break durations, your chosen appearance setting, and whether you have completed the introduction. These records stay on your device and are used only to provide the corresponding features.

Data sharing and internet access

The app does not upload your plans to a server, request location data, or use accounts, advertising trackers, or analytics. Kinoviq does not collect, sell or share personal data with third parties. Movie search and recommendations work without an internet connection.

Children

Kinoviq does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

How to delete your data

You can delete movie nights inside the app. To remove all locally saved settings and plans, uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app's features or data-handling practices change, this policy will be updated on this page.
