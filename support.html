Quick answers

  • Does Kinoviq need the internet? No. The movie catalog is built into the app, so search, Movie Time, Movie Picker and the planner all work offline.
  • Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up or login.
  • How do I delete my movie nights? Open a plan and tap Delete. Uninstalling the app removes all locally saved data.
  • How do I switch between dark and light? Tap the gear on the Home screen and choose a theme.

When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model and iOS version.
